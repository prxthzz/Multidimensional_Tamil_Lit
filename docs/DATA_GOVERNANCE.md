# Data & Copyright Governance Policy

## 1. Corpus License Categories
Every literary work and translation in the platform is tagged with explicit copyright states:
- `PUBLIC_DOMAIN`: Classical Sangam literature (Thirukkural, Thiruvarutpa).
- `LICENSED`: Authorized commercial/academic translations.
- `INSTITUTIONAL`: Proprietary institutional datasets.
- `RESTRICTED`: Internal research restricted documents.

## 2. Provenance & Versioning
Every persisted evidence record stores `corpus_version`, `embedding_version`, `retriever_version`, `reranker_version`, and `llm_version`.
