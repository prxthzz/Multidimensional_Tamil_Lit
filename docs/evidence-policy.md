# Evidence & Relationship Policy

## Canonical Relationship Taxonomy

1. **DIRECT:** The literary verse explicitly describes an observation closely related to the modern concept.
2. **STRONG_ANALOGY:** The verse and scientific concept share a meaningful cause-effect pattern or conceptual structure.
3. **THEMATIC:** The passage concerns the same broad topic or domain.
4. **METAPHORICAL:** The relationship is symbolic, poetic, or figurative.
5. **UNSUPPORTED:** The connection cannot be adequately justified.

## Evidence Levels

- **HIGH:** Direct verse description with traditional commentary support.
- **MEDIUM:** Strong analogy or thematic alignment in authentic corpus.
- **LOW:** Symbolic/metaphorical relationship.
- **NONE:** No valid relationship.

## Hallucination Protection Policy
Banned phrases (e.g. "discovered astrophysics", "predicted modern science", "proved relativity") are automatically intercepted and replaced with evidence-grounded expressions ("shares thematic parallels with", "can be conceptually compared with").
